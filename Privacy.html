Privacy Policy — Orbital

Effective Date: May 13, 2026 | App: Orbital (Code in Orbit) | App ID: 2096054051003939

1. Overview

Orbital is an educational PWA game that teaches programming. This policy explains how we handle data when you use Facebook / Instagram Login.

2. Data We Collect

  • Via Facebook Login (App 2096054051003939): When you choose "Continue with Facebook", we receive your public profile: name, email address (if you grant permission), and profile picture via Facebook Graph API.
  • Via Instagram Login: Basic Instagram profile if you connect.
  • Locally Stored: Game progress, points, certificates, and user object are stored in your browser's localStorage (`orbital_user`, `orbital_points`). Nothing is sent to our servers.

3. How We Use Data

  • To personalize your learning experience (show your name/avatar)
  • To track points and certificates locally
  • To enable login and logout

We do NOT sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties for advertising.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data stays in your browser. There is no backend database. If you clear localStorage or logout, your local data is removed. Facebook Login tokens are managed by Facebook SDK and expire per Facebook policy.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook/Instagram): Governed by Meta's Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/
  • GitHub Pages: Hosting provided by GitHub, governed by GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Your Rights

You can: Logout anytime (removes local profile), clear browser storage, revoke app access at facebook.com → Settings → Apps and Websites → Remove Orbital, request deletion by email.

7. Children

Orbital is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's data.
